Testing procedure

  1. Verify patient details and collect a labelled sample.
  2. Dip the strip briefly in urine.
  3. Wait for the recommended reaction time.
  4. Read pads using the system and record results.

Urine analytes

  • Glucose
  • Protein
  • Ketones
  • Blood
  • Bilirubin
  • Nitrite
  • Leukocytes
  • pH
  • Specific Gravity
  • Urobilinogen

Healthcare stakeholders

  • Doctor
    • Reviews findings and advises care.
  • Laboratory Technician
    • Prepares samples and runs the test.
  • Patient
    • Provides sample and receives report.
  • Hospital Administrator
    • Manages records and services.

Abbreviations

UTI
Urinary Tract Infection.
RBC
Red Blood Cells.
WBC
White Blood Cells.
SG
Specific Gravity, indicating urine concentration.
