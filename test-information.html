Diagnostic process

A reagent strip is dipped into a fresh urine sample and read by the system. Important parameters such as glucose, protein and blood help support clinical screening.

Always collect samples hygienically and consult a qualified clinician about abnormal results.

Glucose may be measured in mg/dL; acidity is expressed as pH and concentration as SG. Chemical notation includes H2O and CO2; a reference value can be written as 102 cells/µL.

UTI, RBC, WBC, pH and SG are common report abbreviations.
