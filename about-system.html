Objectives

Provide a quick, consistent way to record urine strip observations and share them with healthcare teams.

Working Principle

Colour changes on reagent pads are compared with reference values. The reader converts those changes into easy-to-read results.

Benefits

It supports faster screening, organised reports and better patient communication.
